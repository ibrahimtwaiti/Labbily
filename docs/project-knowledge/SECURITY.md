# Security and operational governance
Requirements: authenticated tenant and RBAC checks on each endpoint/job, CSRF, secure session cookies, password hashing and login throttles, secret encryption at rest and TLS, webhook signatures, replay/dedup protection, least privilege, secure private file/scanner access, tenant-safe AI context, prompt-injection defense and tool authorization, audit trail, rate limits and quotas. Tests must include unauthorized and cross-tenant cases, role downgrade, concurrent updates, expiry and replays. Never claim full compliance from tests.
Historical V1 decisions documented in governance: new passwords minimum 16 characters, 30 min idle and 12h absolute session, MFA not required for V1 owner exception, retention policy and legal holds. Review current code before applying; do not weaken controls based on historical docs. Historical exposed-key exception is not safe; rotate and verify scope before commercial data. Do not include secret values or private contact details.
Legal: Saudi PDPL as applicable, DPA, controller/processor responsibilities, verified entity, cross-border transfer assessment, data-subject export/deletion, legal holds, deletion ledger after restoration, retention and backups. Historical notes refer to Frankfurt and R2 location; verify current location and legal advice before commercial launch. V1 governance and V2 encrypted restore differ in scope; confirm latest backup policy. No real customer data in test. No production access or outbound communications without owner authorization.
